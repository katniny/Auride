## System Requirements
- Windows 10 1803 or later:
    - CPU: x86_64 only, ARM64 coming soon
- macOS 15 or later:
    - CPU: Apple Silicon only
- Linux:
    - OS: Any Linux distro supporting WebKit2GTK 4.1 (e.g., Ubuntu 22.04)
    - CPU: x86_64 only, ARM64 coming soon
- Android 12 or later:
    - CPU: ARM64, ARMV7, x86_64 or x86
