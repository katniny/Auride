## System Requirements
- Windows:
    - OS: Windows 10 1803 or newer, any Windows 11 version
    - CPU: x86_64 only, ARM64 coming soon
- macOS:
    - OS: macOS 15 or later
    - CPU: Apple Silicon only
- Linux:
    - OS: Ubuntu 22.04 or later
    - CPU: x86_64 only, ARM64 coming soon
- Android:
    - OS: Android 12 or later
    - CPU: x86_64 or ARM64
